# Fictional sale fixtures

The canonical `corpus.ts` deterministically generates **176 private fictional records** using seed `20261004` and clock `2026-10-04`: 144 normal variation records from 48 contributors with three lots each, then 32 stress records. Normal partitions contain 96 training records from 32 contributors, 24 development records from eight contributors, and 24 held-out test records from eight contributors. Template and scenario families carry distinct partition IDs; copied lots inherit their original partition. Initial publication uses only ordinary training records.

The generator-only factors `s/h/b/t` concern sorting, storage humidity, packaging and collection access. Their declared price equation is `log(p)=log(6000)+0.12*s-0.08*h+0.05*b-0.04*t+epsilon`, with seeded uniform noise in [-0.035,0.035], and final prices rounded to 50 TZS/kg. Factors generate varied available descriptions and missing observations. They are never required farmer input or features supplied directly to a deployed embedding model. Derived comparability labels carry `synthetic_unreviewed`, and do not establish agricultural validation.

`generate.ts` also retains the earlier 96-record arithmetic/legacy publication fixture for focused regression tests. Its four fixture strata are test generation choices; learned publication groups must be discovered from the current corpus evidence vectors.

Every person, cooperative, attestation and price is invented. Core net prices are `6000` (sorted) or `5200` (mixed), plus `200` for medium lots, plus `(index % 7 - 3) * 100`, in TZS/kg. Quantities and dates use a seeded linear congruential generator. USD originals use the explicitly synthetic 2500 TZS/USD rate; INR/USD=80 and KES/USD=130 exercise cross-rates. These are arithmetic fixtures, not market data or current exchange rates.

The four final challenges include legitimate 1500 and 14000 TZS/kg prices, a 300000 TZS / 50 kg sale with 60000 loan settlement, and an 80 kg boundary lot. Duplicate lots cannot earn credit or weight. Rejected missing/conflicting facts remain private test cases.

Fixtures are generated by the implementation team, released under CC0-1.0, and not independently reviewed. The source fixture module is for server/training use only. Browsers receive validated aggregate packs, never this record array.
